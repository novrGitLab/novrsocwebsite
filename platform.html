THE FULL PICTURE

Everything your SOC needs,
in one platform.

Nine connected capabilities working as one system, not nine disconnected tools.

LIVE◉

Real-time threat detection

SIEM monitoring correlates every endpoint around the clock against tens of thousands of detection rules, with MITRE ATT&CK mapping on every alert and automatic case creation for anything that matters.

  • SSH brute-force detection
  • Malware behavioural analysis
  • Lateral movement tracking
LIVE⟳

SOAR automation

A tiered response pipeline: low-severity alerts enrich and auto-close; mid-tier alerts trigger a recommended containment action — such as blocking the source IP — that runs under a pre-approved policy or waits for analyst approval, configurable per tenant; critical alerts escalate to the CISO.

  • Automatic case creation
  • Policy-gated or analyst-approved containment
  • CISO escalation on critical alerts
LIVE⌖

Nigerian threat intelligence

A live threat heatmap across all 36 states and the FCT, built on real scanning telemetry, with ngCERT, NCC-CSIRT and CBN advisory integration.

  • Live Nigerian ASN telemetry
  • ngCERT advisory feed
  • State-level threat heatmap
LIVE⬡

Multi-source CTI platform

Enrich any IP, domain, hash or URL across nine intelligence sources at once, with cached results and STIX 2.1 export for sharing with partners and regulators.

  • 9 simultaneous sources
  • STIX 2.1 export
  • IOC history and caching
LIVE◈

Brand protection suite

Monitor domains, social media, app stores and code repositories. Detect typosquatting, impersonation and credential leaks in real time.

  • Domain lookalike detection
  • Executive breach monitoring
  • Source code leak scanning
LIVE✉

Email security

DMARC, SPF and DKIM monitoring, AI-assisted phishing detection, real-time blocklist checking and full email header analysis.

  • DMARC / SPF / DKIM checks
  • AI phishing classifier
  • Real-time blocklist checking
AI✦

NovrAI security analyst

An AI security analyst on call around the clock, grounded in your live platform data rather than generic advice.

  • Case triage guidance
  • MITRE technique mapping
  • Compliance Q&A (NDPA / CBN)
LIVE✓

Nigerian compliance

Track NDPA, the CBN Cybersecurity Framework, NCC, ISO/IEC 27001 and PCI-DSS. Auto-generate evidence reports for auditors.

  • 5 compliance frameworks
  • Control-level assessment
  • Auditor evidence export
BETA⌘

MSSP multi-tenancy

Manage multiple client organisations from one dashboard, with per-org data isolation and role-based access across admin, SOC manager, analyst and executive roles.

  • Per-org data isolation
  • Guided client onboarding
  • Role-based navigation
